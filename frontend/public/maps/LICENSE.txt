MENTORX Geography: base map outlines (frontend/public/maps/*.json)

Maps are simplified for practice and do not show official international boundaries.

1. Map outlines
The outlines are derived (rounded coordinates, labels and place codes removed) from the SVG maps
of the open-source slepemapy.cz application, https://github.com/adaptive-learning/geography
(geography/static/map/), used under the MIT licence below.
